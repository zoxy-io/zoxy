**A fix release for HTTP/2, which 0.10.0 introduced.** If you turned
HTTP/2 on, upgrade: 0.10.0 can abort on an upload in two ways, below.
If you did not, nothing here reaches you. Nothing in your config has to
change, and the shipped binary prices every budget exactly as 0.10.0 did.

## An upload could take the process down (#373)

**Who is affected.** A listener that speaks HTTP/2, over TLS by `tls.alpn`
or in the clear by `http.h2c`, running 0.10.0.

**What happened.** A client's DATA frame is held when its stream's relay
buffer has no room for it. That is ordinary. A full 16 KiB frame of a body
sent without a `content-length` overruns the default 16 KiB buffer by the
58 octets its chunk framing needs. Larger frames sent before the client
applies the server's window overrun it too. While a frame is held, nothing
else on its connection is read.

Suppose, while a frame is held, both of these happen:

- the connection is going away: it reached `keepalive_requests` (1,000 by
  default), the server is draining, or the client said GOAWAY;
- the origin answers in full before it has read the body, as with a 401 or
  a 413.

Then the answer ends the connection's last stream, and the session closes
with the rest of the frame still held. The next step of the connection hit
an assertion. Assertions stay on in the shipped ReleaseSafe build, so the
process aborted, and every connection it held went with it.

**The fix.** A held frame whose session has closed is dropped with the
session. Nothing is left to take its octets, and no window is left to
credit them to.

## An upload that ended exactly at its buffer's room aborted the process

**Who is affected.** The same listeners, on any upload sent without a
`content-length`.

**What happened.** Such a body goes to the origin chunked, so the proxy
keeps room behind what it has gathered. That room holds the CRLF that
ends a chunk, plus either the next chunk's size line or the last chunk.
Suppose the body's final octets fill that room exactly. Then one chunk
carries both the CRLF and the last chunk, and the proxy computed where a
next chunk would start: a few octets past the buffer. No octet was
written there, but an assertion caught the overrun and the process
aborted. One DATA frame was enough: 986 octets ending the body against
the smallest relay buffer, 16,346 against the default, if it arrived
while the origin was still being dialed. The last piece of a held frame,
or several frames together, could fill the room just as exactly.

**The fix.** The last chunk has no next one, so no room is kept for one.
The window each stream advertises is unchanged.

## A body could be forwarded for an exchange its deadline had ended

One more held-frame case, found once the simulator could reach it.
A held frame on one stream stops its connection's other streams, by design,
until it drains.

Suppose one deadline pass ends two of those streams at once: one still
dialing, and one with its body half sent. The first stream's 504 can then
unblock the connection before the second's ending has settled. The second
stream's next frame was forwarded to an upstream that had just been shut
down to end it. The send failed and the 504 still went out, so clients
saw no difference. But body octets were being forwarded for an exchange
already being answered. They are now dropped and credited to the client.

## Checked against

- **The simulator now sends HTTP/2 request bodies.** Before, its HTTP/2
  clients sent only GETs, so no seed ever held a frame, and the nightly
  soak could not have found any of these.
  - Half of a client's streams now POST a body, with its length declared
    or not, in up to four DATA frames multiplexed across streams.
  - The origin checks every octet against what the client sent.
  - A third of adversarial seeds draw a relay buffer from 1 KiB up, so
    frames are held.
  - With the first fix reverted, every 50,000-seed shard of a sweep finds
    the crash within about 10,000 seeds.
  - The deadline bug was found by the first sweep past the nightly's
    range, and the exact-room abort by the first nightly run with bodies.
- **Bed tests.** Each fix has a directed test that aborts without it.
- **The soak.** A nightly run on this release's code passed: 4 × 1,000,000
  seeds, [run 43716315443](https://github.com/zoxy-io/zoxy/actions/runs/43716315443).
- **What the session relies on is now asserted (#374).** A connection whose
  session ends between steps is closed by the completion of a send the
  ending itself started. That always held. It is now asserted where it is
  relied on, rather than reasoned about.

## Upgrading

Replace the binary. No config key, metric name, counter or default moved.
`--check` prices the same configs as 0.10.0.
